Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn’t here, write to us.

Writing

How do chapters and volumes work?

Each day is a chapter, numbered by the day of the year (13 November is Chapter 317). Each month is a volume. When a month ends, its chapters appear together as a volume spine on your Shelf.

How do I save a chapter?

Pick a mood, write as much or as little as you like, then tap Ink this chapter. The button stays grey until you’ve picked a mood.

Can I edit an earlier day?

Yes. Open the chapter from Home or the Shelf and tap the pen button to rewrite it. You can have one chapter per day.

How do photos work?

Tap Add photo on the writing page. You can add one photo per chapter. It’s converted to black-and-white screentone, and only that converted copy is saved.

Settings

How do I change or turn off the daily reminder?

Open Settings (the gear on Home) and use Daily reminder to turn it on or off and pick a time. If reminders never arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Journalink in iOS Settings → Notifications.

How does the lock work, and what if Face ID fails?

Turn on Lock in Settings and Journalink hides your journal whenever you leave the app. If Face ID or Touch ID doesn’t recognise you, you can unlock with your device passcode instead.

Can I switch between light and dark pages?

Yes. In Settings → Night mode, choose Auto (follows your device), Day (white paper) or Night (black paper).

Your data

How do I export my journal?

Settings → Export all entries creates a plain-text file with every chapter, grouped by volume. Save it to Files or send it wherever you like. Photos aren’t included in the text file.

How do I move my journal to a new iPhone or iPad?

Journalink has no account or cloud sync. Your journal moves with your device backup: restore your new device from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. To keep a copy of your words regardless, export them first.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the Journalink app. That removes your journal and settings from the device. We don’t hold a copy, so deleted entries can’t be recovered.

Does Journalink collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
