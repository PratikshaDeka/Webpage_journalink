Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Journalink does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you write stays on your device.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No servers: we never receive your entries, moods or photos.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party code that collects data.

What stays on your device

Your chapters (text, mood and date), any photo you attach, and your settings are saved only in the app’s storage on your iPhone or iPad. Journalink has no internet connection of its own and does not send this information anywhere.

Photos

When you add a photo, you choose it with Apple’s photo picker, so Journalink only ever sees the single photo you pick, never your library. The app converts it to a black-and-white screentone image and saves only that converted copy. Location and camera details are not kept.

Face ID and passcode lock

If you turn on the lock, unlocking is handled entirely by iOS. Journalink only learns whether unlocking succeeded. It never sees or stores your face data or passcode.

Reminders

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. It is not sent from a server, and you can turn it off anytime in the app’s Settings or in iOS Settings.

Export

“Export all entries” creates a plain-text file on your device and opens the share sheet. The file only goes where you choose to send or save it.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include Journalink’s data in those backups, as it does for most apps. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by Journalink.

Deleting your data

Deleting the Journalink app removes your journal and settings from the device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted entries, so export first if you want to keep them.

Children

Journalink collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
